From Experiment to Film

Every film tonight came out of the same generator. The difference is five decisions, made before the first clip.

1
Style
Pick one look and lock it in writing. Every clip repeats the same style sentence, word for word — if it isn't written down, it drifts.
2
Camera
Decide whose eyes we're in. One camera logic per film: a housesitter's phone, a crane that never blinks, a stage built for clay. The camera is a character.
3
Lighting
One believable light source per scene — streetlamp, phone screen, porch light. Darkness is free production design.
4
Audio
Sound is half the film. Voices are recorded inside the clip, never pasted on after — and one low soundbed carries every cut.
5
References
Anchor people and props to a picture, not adjectives. One reference portrait kept the same woman in 49 clips.
