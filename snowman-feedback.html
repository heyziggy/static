About the film

George the snowman stands in the yard all winter and watches the house live its season — the lights going up one string at a time, the storm, the fireworks, the lights coming down again. He never moves. He sees it all from a still position in the yard. It is, at once, loving and lonely.

Made with generative AI video, a human-written script and edit, narration, and a Kevin MacLeod score. Submitted to Cue Christmas ’26.
